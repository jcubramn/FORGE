Your data in FORGE

Updated September 21, 2026

What the app stores

Google sign-in supplies your account identifier, email and basic profile information. FORGE stores the profile details you enter, including age, height, weight, workout preferences, exercise exclusions, diet and goals, alongside workout history and weight check-ins. You can skip calorie estimates. Avoid entering medical records or other sensitive details in free-text notes.

How it is used

These details personalize your training plan and optional calorie estimates, display progress, and restore your history when you sign in. FORGE does not request access to your Gmail, Google Drive or contacts.

Storage and services

Supabase provides sign-in and database storage. Account records are restricted to the signed-in account using database access policies. Vercel hosts the app. Google handles Google authentication. These providers process requests and may retain service and security logs under their own policies. Your authentication session is kept in this browser tab's session storage. Earlier local previews may retain separate data in browser local storage.

Your choices

You can edit your profile, export your FORGE data and sign out from the profile screen. Account data remains stored until removed. For questions or to request deletion of your FORGE account data, contact jcubramn@gmail.com from your account email. Revoking Google access does not by itself delete existing FORGE records.
